03 / OUR STORY

Come hungry.
Leave happy.

Aroma White started in Texas in 2023 with a simple idea: create a welcoming neighborhood café where great food, warm spaces, and everyday moments come together.

We believe good food should be accessible, delicious, and thoughtfully prepared — familiar enough for every day, with enough character to make the moment feel special.

07 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few little questions.

This is a fictional portfolio café, so real opening hours are not published. A production version could show live business hours here.
